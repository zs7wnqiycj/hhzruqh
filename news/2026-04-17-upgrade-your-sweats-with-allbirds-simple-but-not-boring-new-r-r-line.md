# Upgrade Your Sweats With Allbirds' Simple (But Not Boring) New R&R Line

Our product picks are editor-tested, expert-approved. We may earn a commission through links on our site. Why Trust Us?

Let's be honest: Even if the world is regaining some sort of normalcy, you're still living in loungewear. And there's no reason to be ashamed about it—especially when those sweats come from an eco-conscious brand and look this good.

Allbirds, the men's clothing brand known for its famed Dasher performance shoe, just launched its new causal R&R Collection, which features loungewear for men that you can actually wear outside of the house. The simple (but not boring) line, launched on Nov. 1, includes a crewneck-style sweatshirt, a standard hoodie, and a pair of relaxed sweatpants. All the pieces are effortless, laid-back, and perfect basics to add to your wardrobe.

[Simple But](https://reboundworks.escortskhi.com/)

Each piece comes in five colorways, two of which are in the core collection. The other three colors are limited-edition hues that are here for launch but won't be around forever. In the core line is a Natural Black color and a Natural White hue for the neutral sweat set of your dreams. The fall-ready limited-edition line includes a saturated pine green, a true navy, and a trendy terracotta tone.

The loungewear collection is made of a sustainable blend of organic Pima cotton, TENCEL, and hemp, which create a soft, comfortable fabric with added durability. And the sizing is likely more inclusive than you're used to, with all pieces available in extra small to an XXL. The luxe pieces do come with a luxe price tag—$98 for the sweatpants and sweatshirt, and $118 for the hoodie, to be exact. But Allbirds' top-notch quality guarantees you won't be disappointed.

Netflix announced a new prequel series to The Witcher, titled The Witcher: Blood Origin is in the works.

The show will focus on the origin of the first Witcher.

There's no premiere date yet, but the show will film in the U.K.

It looks like fans of The Witcher just got lucky, because after one season on Netflix, the Henry Cavill-led series is now expanding its universe to include a prequel.

The streamer announced Monday that a six-part, live-action limited series is currently in the works, with its setting in the same universe as the current show. Titled The Witcher: Blood Origin, the prequel will travel 1,200 years prior to the events of Geralt of Rivia's journey, focusing on the beginnings of the first Witcher and a “‘conjunction of the spheres,’ when the worlds of monsters, men and elves merged to become one.”

“As a lifelong fan of fantasy, I am beyond excited to tell the story The Witcher: Blood Origin,” executive producer/showrunner Declan de Barra said in a statement Monday about the upcoming series. “A question has been burning in my mind ever since I first read The Witcher books – What was the Elven world really like before the cataclysmic arrival of the humans? I’ve always been fascinated by the rise and fall of civilizations, how science, discovery, and culture flourish right before that fall.”

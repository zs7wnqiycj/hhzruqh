Explanation

[Sleep Apnea Damages Your](https://regeneration-desk.hbsnzj.com/blog/8453030751.html)

Sleep apnea is when you stop breathing for short times while you sleep. This can be bad for your heart and blood pressure.

How Sleep Apnea Affects Your Heart

Heart Strain: When you stop breathing, your heart has to work extra hard to get oxygen to your body.

Irregular Heartbeats: Sleep apnea can cause your heart to beat in a funny way, which is not healthy.

Blood Pressure

Increases Blood Pressure: Sleep apnea can make your blood pressure go up. High blood pressure is not good because it can hurt your heart and blood vessels.

Why It Matters

Having sleep apnea can make you more likely to have heart problems, like heart attacks or heart failure.

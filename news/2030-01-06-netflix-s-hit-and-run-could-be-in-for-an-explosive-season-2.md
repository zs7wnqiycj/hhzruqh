# Netflix's 'Hit and Run' Could Be in for an Explosive Season 2

Season 1 of Netflix's Hebrew-English espionage drama Hit and Run ended with Segev Azulai (played by Lior Raz) sprinting west across the George Washington Bridge, a Mossad agent dead behind him, his daughter missing, and Segev inexplicably risking his life just to cross into New Jersey (Take a cab, my dude.). How a former special operative traveled from Tel Aviv into the Garden State obviously wasn’t the season’s only mystery or cliffhanger—which entail several unresolved events back in New York and across the world in Israel.

[for an Explosive](https://barrierwatch.bbsxyxy.com/blog/4016678601.html)

Netflix assembled a stacked team to bring this original concept to life, including Avi Issacharoff and Lior Raz (who created the hit Israeli drama Fauda) alongside Dawn Prestwich and Nicole Yorkin (The Killing). The result—a nine episode international spy thriller involving two intelligence agencies, a newsroom with very questionable editorial standards, and what seems to be a Trump-esque White House doing some treasonous shit. It’s a drama somewhere between junky crowd favorites like Who Killed Sarah? and popcorn dad favorites like the Bourne series.

While the first season sets up several tangled storylines and agency plots, viewers won’t find too much closure in the finale. Must mean a second season, right? Right?

Will there be a Season 2 of Hit and Run?

While there’s no official word from Netflix (they’ll likely wait for the viewership numbers to roll in), internet rumors suggest Lior Raz plans to start filming this fall. Netflix previously acquired Raz’s show Fauda, which became a massive international hit. It’s possible Raz was given a multiple season deal for Hit and Run, which might explain its cliffhangers.

We should find out officially in the coming weeks.

Well, there’s no dearth of unanswered questions; the series could easily keep this mystery train going for another couple seasons. For one, we still don’t fully understand the spy games between the CIA and Mossad—why, for instance, both intelligence agencies are actively involved on each other’s turf, why both seem to want the same piece of intelligence suppressed, what else they’ve been keeping from the public, who else they’ve been killing, and how Segev can literally walk through Times Square and not be seen by either.

Segev's daughter, Ella, has also been kidnapped—perhaps turning season 2 from Bourne into Taken. And then there’s Danielle Wexler (aka Sophie Dreyer), who in, like, one year, somehow went from college student dancing in NYC to undercover CIA operative stabbing a dude to death in a Tel Aviv parking lot.

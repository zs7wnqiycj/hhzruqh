Can I take ibuprofen and acetaminophen together?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

[and Acetaminophen](https://lumina-dermis.sunsimiaosd.com/)

Yes, ibuprofen and acetaminophen can be safely taken together for pain relief, and research shows the combination often works better than either medication alone. This is because they work through different mechanisms: ibuprofen (an NSAID) reduces inflammation, while acetaminophen acts on pain signals in the brain. Because they are processed differently by the body, taking them together does not increase the risk of overdose when standard doses are followed. Many doctors recommend this combination for moderate pain such as headaches, dental pain, or muscle soreness.

However, pain can have many underlying causes, and reaching for medication without understanding the source may delay proper treatment.

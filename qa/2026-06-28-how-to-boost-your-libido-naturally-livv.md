Why Low Libido Matters

Low libido can be more than just a lack of desire—it may indicate an underlying health issue. Libido is influenced by a mix of hormones, mental health, lifestyle habits, and environmental factors. A consistently low sex drive could point to hormonal imbalances, chronic stress, or a nutritional deficiency.

Causes of Low Libido

To address low libido effectively, it’s essential to identify the root causes. Here are some factors to consider:

Hormonal imbalances: Low testosterone, estrogen fluctuations (especially during menopause or postpartum), and thyroid issues can affect libido.

Chronic stress: Elevated cortisol levels suppress sex hormones and reduce libido.

Dietary habits: A diet high in processed foods and sugar can lead to deficiencies that impact hormone production.

Sleep quality: Poor sleep lowers testosterone levels and energy, reducing libido.

Environmental toxins: Chemicals in plastics, pesticides, and personal care products can disrupt hormone regulation.

Chronic conditions: Conditions such as diabetes, cardiovascular disease, and autoimmune disorders can impair sexual function.

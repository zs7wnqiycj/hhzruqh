Explanation

Fertility issues in women can arise from several causes. One common issue is problems with ovulation, which means the egg is not released from the ovary properly. This can make it hard to get pregnant.

Another issue is blocked fallopian tubes. The fallopian tubes are pathways for the egg to meet the sperm, and if they are blocked, fertilization cannot occur.

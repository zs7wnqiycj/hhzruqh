# Warning: Are Your Meds Worsening RBD? Urgent Doctor's Note | Ubie Doctor's Note

Can certain medications trigger or worsen REM Sleep Behavior Disorder (RBD)?

[RBD Urgent](https://barrierwatch.bbsxyxy.com/blog/1084532180.html)

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Certain medications—especially antidepressants such as SSRIs, SNRIs, and tricyclics—can trigger or worsen REM Sleep Behavior Disorder (RBD), a condition where people physically act out vivid dreams during sleep. Beta-blockers and some other drugs may also contribute. If you've noticed new symptoms after starting a medication, it's important not to stop it on your own—instead, identify what may be going on so you can discuss next steps with your doctor.

Understanding RBD:

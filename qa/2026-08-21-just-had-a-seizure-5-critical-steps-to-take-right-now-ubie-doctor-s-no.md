# Just Had a Seizure? 5 Critical Steps to Take Right Now | Ubie Doctor's Note

Published on: 7/21/2026

What should I do immediately after having a seizure?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

After a seizure, stay calm, rest in a safe position, and avoid sudden activity. Turn onto your side to keep your airway clear, and note the time and details of what happened. Rehydrate, eat something light, and allow your body to recover, as fatigue and confusion are common. Contact a doctor immediately if it was your first seizure, if it lasted longer than 5 minutes, if another seizure followed, or if you were injured.

Understanding why a seizure occurred is just as important as recovering from one. Causes can range from epilepsy and low blood sugar to sleep deprivation, medication reactions, or underlying neurological conditions — and identifying the trigger early can prevent recurrence. Take a free, instant, online symptom check to clarify possible causes, understand urgency, and confidently plan your next steps.

Explanation

After a seizure, it is important to take care of yourself and ensure you are safe. Here are some steps you can follow:

Stay Calm and Rest: After a seizure, you may feel tired or confused. It is important to rest and let your body recover.

[Seizure Critical](https://antimicrobial-hub.sunsimiaosd.com/blog/8765372368.html)

Ensure Safety: Make sure you are in a safe place where you cannot hurt yourself. If you are not alone, ask someone to stay with you until you feel better.

Seek Medical Advice: If this is your first seizure, it is important to seek medical advice. An urgent EEG may be recommended to understand the cause of the seizure. You can start by using a free symptom checker to help describe your symptoms before your medical appointment.

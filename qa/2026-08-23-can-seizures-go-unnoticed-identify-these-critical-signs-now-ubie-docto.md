Yes, seizures can occur without being noticed, particularly if they are mild, brief, or happen during sleep. Known as "silent" or subclinical seizures, these episodes may cause subtle symptoms like brief staring spells, momentary confusion, twitching, or unusual sensations that are easy to overlook. Nocturnal seizures often go undetected entirely, with the only clues being morning fatigue, a bitten tongue, or unexplained bedwetting.

Because unrecognized seizures can affect memory, mood, and safety over time, identifying them early is important.

Explanation

Narcolepsy and regular sleepiness might seem similar, but they are quite different. Narcolepsy is a medical condition that causes people to suddenly fall asleep, even during activities like driving. This is not just feeling tired; it can happen without warning and is hard to control. Regular sleepiness, on the other hand, usually happens because someone didn't get enough sleep or is tired from doing a lot during the day. It doesn't usually cause sudden sleep attacks.

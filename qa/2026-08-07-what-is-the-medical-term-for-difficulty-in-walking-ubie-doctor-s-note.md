What is the medical term for difficulty in walking?

"Gait disturbance" is the medical term for difficulty in walking. This term describes any abnormality in a person's walking pattern.

Gait disturbance is a medical term for when someone has trouble walking normally. It encompasses various walking problems, including changes in walking patterns after an injury like a sprained ankle or difficulty walking after a stroke. This medical term allows doctors to describe and discuss walking difficulties before even identifying their underlying cause.

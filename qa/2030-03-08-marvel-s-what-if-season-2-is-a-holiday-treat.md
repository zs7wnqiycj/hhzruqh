HAPPY HOLIDAYS! WHILE 2023 for Marvel Studios has been a year plagued, unquestionably, with less success than any in recent memory, that's not going to stop them from leaving fans with an exciting end-of-calendar gift to close things out: What If...? Season 2.

If it feels like it's been a long time since Season 1 of What If...? came out, well, that's because it has been. Since the inaugural season of hypothetical animated superhero happenings concluded in October 2021, there have been eight MCU feature films and six seasons of MCU Disney+ television. A lot to keep up with!

But the ever-expansive (and, for some, tiring) nature of the MCU plays wonderfully into What If...? which, with each episode, tells a stand-alone story of something within this world that could have gone a bit differently. If you'll remember back to Season 1, we saw an episode where Peggy Carter became Captain America instead of Steve Rogers, we saw a world where T'Challa became Star-Lord instead of the Black Panther, and we saw our heroes fighting back in a world overrun with zombies... and that's just to name a few.

Residing over everything—trying his hardest to remain impartial, and rarely succeeding—is Uatu, the Watcher (voiced by Jeffrey Wright), an omnipresent being who sees everything in every universe.

As What If...? arrives with nine more episodes—a welcomed holiday treat—we're excited to see what unfolds. Here's a full list of when each episode is coming out—and how you can be sure not to miss a single one.

What time do new episodes of What If...? Season 2 come out?

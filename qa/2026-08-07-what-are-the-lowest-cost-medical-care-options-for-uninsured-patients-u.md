Uninsured patients can access low-cost medical care through several proven options. Community health centers offer comprehensive services on a sliding fee scale based on income. Free clinics, hospital charity care programs, and federally qualified health centers (FQHCs) also provide affordable treatment. Prescription assistance programs, telehealth services, and direct primary care memberships further reduce costs for those without insurance.

Before booking any appointment, it helps to know what condition you may be dealing with and how urgent it is.

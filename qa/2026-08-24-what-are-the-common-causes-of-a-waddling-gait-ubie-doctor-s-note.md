[waddling gait Ubie Doctor's](https://incretin-path.escortskhi.com/)

A waddling gait is most often caused by weakness in the muscles that help control movement in the hip area. This weakness leads to an exaggerated side-to-side movement when walking, creating the characteristic waddling pattern. During pregnancy, a waddling gait is normal as the body adapts to weight distribution changes. However, a waddling gait can also signal several medical conditions, including:

Muscular dystrophy, which causes progressive muscle weakness and can make walking difficult

Hip dysplasia, a condition where the hip joint doesn't form properly

Spinal cord injuries or disorders that affect muscle control

Osteomalacia, a condition where bones become soft and weak due to vitamin D deficiency

Muscle weakness caused by certain autoimmune disorders that affect muscle function

Weight gain which places extra weight and stress on the hip muscles

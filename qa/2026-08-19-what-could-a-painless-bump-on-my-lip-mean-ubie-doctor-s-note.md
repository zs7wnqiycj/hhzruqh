Explanation

A painless bump on your lip, especially on the lower lip, is often caused by a cyst that forms due to a blocked or injured minor salivary gland, called a mucocele. This type of cyst is usually harmless and appears as a soft, bump-like swelling on the lip. Other causes of a painless bump on the lips include fibromas (benign growths of fibrous tissue), lipomas (fatty tissue growths), oral papillomas (caused by the HPV virus), or fordyce spots (enlarged oil glands). A healthcare provider can perform a clinical exam and guide next steps based on their findings and your specific situation.

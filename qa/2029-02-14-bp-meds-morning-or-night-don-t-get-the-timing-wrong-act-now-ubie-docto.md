Should I take my blood pressure medicine in the morning or at night?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Research suggests that taking blood pressure medication at night may lower the risk of heart attack, stroke, and other cardiovascular events for some people, as blood pressure naturally dips during sleep and medication timing can influence this rhythm. However, the ideal time to take your medication depends on your specific diagnosis, the type of drug prescribed, and your daily routine. Some medications work best in the morning, while others are more effective at bedtime. Always follow your doctor's guidance on timing to get the full benefit and avoid side effects.

Published on: 7/21/2026

What could cause asymmetrical swelling of the tonsils?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Asymmetrical tonsil swelling—when one tonsil is noticeably larger than the other—is most often caused by infections such as tonsillitis, strep throat, or a peritonsillar abscess. Less commonly, it can signal more serious conditions, including benign growths or tumors like lymphoma. Because the causes range from mild to serious, a medical evaluation is essential for an accurate diagnosis and timely treatment.

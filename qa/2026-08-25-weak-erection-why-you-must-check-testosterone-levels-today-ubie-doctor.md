What is a weak erection? A weak erection means that the penis does not become hard enough, or stays hard for a long enough time, to support sexual intercourse. It might not feel as firm or reliable as one expects for a successful sexual experience.

Could low testosterone contribute? Testosterone is a hormone important for sexual desire and overall function. When testosterone levels are low, some men may have a reduced interest in sex (libido) or find it harder to get a firm erection. Research has shown that while low testosterone is not the only factor causing weak erections, it can affect the quality of an erection. In addition to hormones, other factors like blood flow problems, stress, or medical conditions might also lead to difficulties with erections.

In summary, a weak erection is generally one that is not fully firm or maintained long enough for sexual activity, and low testosterone can be one part of the complex mix of factors that may lead to this condition.

Weight loss surgery, also known as bariatric surgery, can significantly improve a person's ability to move and perform physical activities. This is because the surgery helps people lose a lot of weight, which reduces stress on their joints and muscles.

Pain Reduction: After surgery, many people experience less pain, especially in their knees and back. This makes walking and other activities much less painful.

Improved Mobility: Losing weight can make it easier to move around. People often find they can walk longer distances and do more activities without getting tired.

Better Physical Functioning: Overall, people who have had weight loss surgery report they can do more things they enjoy, like playing with their kids or going for a walk.

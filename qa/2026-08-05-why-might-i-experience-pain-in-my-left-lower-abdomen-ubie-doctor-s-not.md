Pain in the left lower abdomen, also known as left lower quadrant pain, can happen for several reasons. It can be due to problems with the digestive system, such as diverticulitis, which is an infection or inflammation of small pouches in the intestine. Other possible causes include kidney stones, urinary tract infections, or even issues related to the reproductive organs, like ovarian cysts.

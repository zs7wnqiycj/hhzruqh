Explanation

If you have the flu with fever, stay home until you no longer have a fever for 24 hours while not taking any medications that would lower your fever. If you are diagnosed with the flu but do not have a fever, it is recommended you stay home for 5 days after your symptoms started. You are most contagious the first 3 days of your illness.

If you are unsure whether your symptoms are flu-related, check your symptoms with a free AI-powered assessment to help determine the next best steps for your care.

Explanation

Stomach aches in the morning can be uncomfortable and disruptive. One possible cause is irritable bowel syndrome (IBS), a common digestive issue that can lead to stomach pain, bloating, and changes in bowel habits. IBS symptoms can often be managed with diet changes and stress reduction.

Another potential cause is chronic nausea, which can affect both children and adults. Chronic nausea often involves a persistent feeling of needing to vomit, and it can be linked to various factors, including anxiety or other lifestyle issues.

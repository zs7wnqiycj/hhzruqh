Understanding Ear Infections

An ear infection, medically known as otitis media, is a common condition primarily affecting the middle ear – the space behind the eardrum. It is often seen in children but can occur in people of all ages.

Ear infections can be classified as either viral or bacterial. Viral infections are more frequent and usually resolve on their own. Bacterial infections, typically caused by Streptococcus pneumoniae or Haemophilus influenzae, might require antibiotics.

Symptoms include ear pain, fluid drainage, hearing loss, and fever. The Eustachian tube, responsible for draining fluids from the middle ear, can become blocked, leading to fluid buildup and infection.

Factors such as allergies, colds, and exposure to secondhand smoke can increase susceptibility.

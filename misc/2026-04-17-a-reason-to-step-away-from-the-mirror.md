# A Reason to Step Away From the Mirror

Guys say women spend too much time on their hair, but I know more than a few men who fuss longer than I do. Obsess is more like it. You know who you are. I was chatting with a stylist the other day KMS expert hair stylist Simon Miller and he gave a quick bit of great advice:

[Step Away](https://glycometrix.snaketongueofficial.com/blog/4307710410.html)

“I have my guy clients style their hair with my 10-second rule. When finishing your hair, put product in and stop messing with it after 10 seconds, and walk away from the mirror. Come back before you leave the house and usually you will have just the look you want. If you try to make it look too perfect, it can have a contrived look. Keep it simple, cool, and clean.”

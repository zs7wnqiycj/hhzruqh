Nonhormonal male birth control pills, such as triptonide, may cause side effects including mild digestive issues, fatigue, and temporary changes in energy levels. However, these effects are generally considered mild, manageable, and fully reversible once the medication is discontinued. Unlike hormonal contraceptives, triptonide works without altering testosterone levels, which may reduce the risk of mood changes or long-term hormonal disruption.

Explanation

Nonhormonal male birth control pills are designed to prevent pregnancy without using hormones. One example of this is triptonide. According to the studies, the side effects of these pills are usually mild and can include digestive problems like stomach upset and feeling tired.

Common Side Effects

Digestive Issues: Some men may experience mild stomach upset or discomfort.

Fatigue: Feeling more tired than usual can occur.

Reversibility

The effects of these pills are generally reversible, meaning that once you stop taking them, your body should return to normal. This is important for men who may want to have children in the future.

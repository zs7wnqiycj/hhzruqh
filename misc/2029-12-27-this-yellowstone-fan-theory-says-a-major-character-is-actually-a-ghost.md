# This 'Yellowstone' Fan Theory Says a Major Character Is Actually a Ghost

[Theory Says Major Character](https://47466.com/19p8d4tq/)

The Yellowstone season 4 premiere—comprising the first two episodes of the series, which will run for 10—picked up where season 3 left off: after the brutal attack that left John Dutton shot on the roadside, Kayce Dutton under fire in his office, and Beth Dutton the recipient of an explosive package. In short, it ended with a bang.

[Fan Theory Says Major](https://leanorbit.dns0663.com/blog/1855421168.html)

Season 4 opened with multiple bangs, as Kayce fired back at his attackers, adding to his absurd Call-of-Duty-level body count, before pursuing his father’s attackers by truck and opening fire on them at an intersection. More shooting. More bodies. And, now: lots of fan theories about said shooting and bodies—including one that's making the rounds online involving Kayce, and whether he's actually...a ghost, i.e., dead.

During the roadside shootout, Kayce is shot and keels over before the series moves forward in time somewhere between 2-3 months later: John is just getting out of the hospital, Beth is scarred but alive, and Kayce is sorta not around. We see him in a ghillie suit during John’s return (only John speaks to him) and then again following John into the woods where they talk (again, only John speaks to him).

The time jump coupled with Kayce’s limited screen time have led fans into a theorizing frenzy, with some suggesting that Kayce died on the roadside; he’s only in John’s head now.

The theory notes the absence of the Monica/Tate storyline in the opening episodes. We still don’t know what happened at the bunkhouse during the attack. After the time jump, Monica and Tate are still not accounted for; they were last seen heading to the bunkhouse during the shooting. Since Monica and Tate haven’t yet interacted with Kayce—and only John appears to acknowledge his existence—it’s unclear to fans that Kayce actually returned.

While it seems ludicrous, the theory is worth considering—if only to pass the time before episode 3.

[Yellowstone' Fan Theory Says](https://antimicrobial-hub.sunsimiaosd.com/blog/3225057291.html)

Is Kayce Dutton dead?

Probably not. Still, only John has had robust conversation with Kayce since his return. In episode 2, Kayce stands alongside Rip and John while the ranch hosted Travis, but Rip only responds to Kayce once—and the response could have just been Rip talking.

Fans have sensed an odd vibe on the ranch since the attack on the Duttons. No character has discussed the attack (aside from John and Kayce, who only speak to each other, and cryptically) and we haven’t yet seen all the Duttons interacting together at the same time. This lack of recognized intersubjectivity is likely the result of the writing—we’re made to feel the kind of separation and distance which might follow an act of violence.

But since no one actually died, fans are getting a weird feeling; everyone seems to act as if someone did. (And after being taken by surprise by an armed militia, it seems strange that there would be no casualties.)

Maybe Kayce didn’t die, but someone else did kick it. It’s likely we’ll revisit the attack timeline at some point in future episodes. We're likely to see the events of the bunkhouse again when we revisit Monica and Tate.

Kayce might be out of the woods, but we still don’t know about his family.

# Tom Hanks Checks How Good an Actor Tom Holland Really Is

• Tom Holland appeared alongside Tom Hanks on The Graham Norton Show. • Hanks was telling a story about voice acting, and pulled Holland into an impromptu acting exercise. • Tom Hanks seems like a great acting coach, if anyone's asking.

[How Good an Actor](https://tiancansz.com/nyzi5h/)

There's little doubt that the best thing about British talk shows—Graham Norton's show in particular—is the multiple celebrity guests on the couch at the same time, interacting with one another. That can make sparks fly, and watching Woody lead Spider-Man in an acting exercise is certainly one that stands out.

Promoting Toy Story 4, Tom Hanks was on the couch alongside Tom Holland and Jake Gyllenhaal, there for Spider-Man: Far From Home (Gwyneth Paltrow was there too, but she's not in Spider-Man, as far as anyone knows). Hanks was talking about his voice acting methods when playing Woody in the Toy Story series, before he mentioned an exercise common for young actors. At this point he pulled Holland, 23, into the mix.

Hanks explained the exercise, meant to be a repetitive back and forth. "Would you like more coffee?" he asked our latest Spider-Man, who took a moment to figure out what was happening. "Coffee, coffee, boy do I need some more coffee," he repeated back, with a little nudging from Hanks.

A little back and forth led to Holland being nudged a little bit by the two-time Academy Award winner. "Could you try it with a little more something to it? Because this is a big, important scene" Hanks asked. "OK, Yes, Tom Hanks," Holland responded, clearly incredulous at what was happening.

[Checks How](https://calmvector.dns0663.com/)

A few more "Coffee, Coffee," back and forths later, Hanks finally drew the performance he was looking for from Holland. "Coffee, coffee, PLEASE, TOM HANKS, GIVE ME SOME MORE COFFEE," Holland howled.

"And that's what it's like!" Hanks said. "Bravo, by the way!" he said, leaning in to shake Holland's hand as Paltrow and Gyllenhaal laughed. Gyllenhaal made an astute observation, too: "Why did you do that with an American accent?"

"I can't act in an English accent anymore!" Holland proclaimed. His journey to becoming full-time Peter Parker is clearly well underway.

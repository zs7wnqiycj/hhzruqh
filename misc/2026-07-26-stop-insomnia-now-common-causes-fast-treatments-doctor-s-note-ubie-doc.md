What are the common causes of an inability to sleep and how can it be treated?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Insomnia is difficulty falling asleep, staying asleep, or waking too early. Common causes include stress, anxiety, poor sleep habits, caffeine, medications, or underlying medical conditions like sleep apnea, thyroid issues, or depression. Treatment often includes improving sleep hygiene, relaxation techniques, cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT-I), and in some cases, prescription or over-the-counter sleep aids.

Because insomnia can stem from many different causes—some lifestyle-related, others medical—identifying the root issue is the fastest path to better sleep. A free, instant, online symptom check can help you pinpoint what may be driving your sleep problems and guide you toward the right next steps, whether that's a simple habit change or a conversation with a doctor.

# 5 Rare Photos From the Making of 'Rocky'

Hardcore fans of Rocky will be floored by Taschen's new coffee table book Rocky: The Complete Films. It's a massive 11.4-inch x 17.3-inch, 380-page beast packed with rare photos, shots from Sylvester Stallone's handwritten notebooks, and even a forward by Sly himself. You'll need the eye-of-the-tiger to open your wallet because it's a walloping $1,000!

In the book, Stallone reveals the psychological impact the movies have had on him:

“In [Rocky Balboa], Dixon’s trainer says that until a man—and this means a woman, too—has been through a real baptism of fire, when you’re scared, when you’re hanging on, when someone is hurting you, which is life is hurting you, then you’re really going to see what you’re made of and then you’re going to get the only kind of respect in the world that matters: self-respect. That’s pretty much what my journey has been. This has all been about getting Rocky self-respect, and maybe a little bit of that will wipe off on me."

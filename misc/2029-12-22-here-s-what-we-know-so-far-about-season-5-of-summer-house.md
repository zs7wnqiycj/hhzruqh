# Here's What We Know So Far About Season 5 of 'Summer House'

Even though a pandemic changed the way reality TV was filmed this year, Bravo fans are going to be excited for this piece of news: Summer House, the hit series following a group of New Yorkers partying in their Hamptons getaway home, is coming back for Season 5—and it looks like a lot of tea (or in this case, frozen margaritas) is ready to be spilled.

[About Season of](https://defensecascade.china-akan.com/blog/3583824485.html)

Though previous seasons of the show followed its cast as they work during the week in NYC and party during the weekends, the network had to change its rules to ensure COVID-19 protocols were in place. The result? When the show got the green light to begin production in July, cast members were expected to quarantine together for the entirety of the summer, meaning more drama, more fights, and more time probably spent in the pantry.

Here’s what we know so far about Season 5 of Summer House, including a trailer, spoilers, and more.

[So Far About](https://tissue-renewal.china-akan.com/blog/6783886811.html)

Who’s starring in Season 5 of Summer House?

[Season of Summer House'](https://copperglow.dns0663.com/)

Fans can expect Lindsay Hubbard, Carl Radke, Hannah Berner, Paige DeSorbo, Luke Gulbranson, and Danielle Olivera to make their return to the Hamptons, along with resident engaged couple Kyle Cooke and Amanda Batula.

ICU nurse Ciara Miller joins the crew for their time in their getaway home. And given her apparent history with Luke coming into the new season, there’s bound to be some kind of drama between the two and Luke's former flame, Hannah.

Are there any spoilers?

Based on the trailer alone, we know cast member Carl Radke is sober and made the decision to become sober after a rather tumultuous last season. The biggest tease of all comes from Kyle and Amanda, as the trailer teases some wedding bells. “What if we just, like, eloped?” Kyle tells his future bride, before the scene immediately cuts to all the houseguests dressed in white.

While we can’t know for sure what’s going to happen, it will definitely become a summer both cast and fans will remember.

When will Season 5 of Summer House premiere?

[About Season of Summer](https://bioresearch.wiki/oxtgot2g/)

The wait is almost over. According to Bravo, Season 5 is set to premiere on February 4th, 2021 at 9PM ET. And you’re in luck, the trailer for the new season is already out.

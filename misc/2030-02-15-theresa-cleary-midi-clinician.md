# Theresa Cleary, Midi Clinician

[Theresa Cleary Midi](https://appetitecode.jcesqc8.com/blog/2262888219.html)

Theresa Cleary, MSN, APRN, FNP-C

Care beyond the usual milestones: "I’ve spent my career in emergency care and family medicine, with a focus on children, adolescents, women, and patients navigating mental health, addiction, and experiences like sexual and domestic violence. I became a nurse and later a nurse practitioner driven by a desire to deliver knowledgeable and compassionate care to all humans. Over time, I began to see a clear gap. So much of women’s care is centered around pregnancy, but what about every other stage of life?"

No one walks alone: "I often tell my patients, ‘I’m not sure if I’m meeting you at your worst, your best, or somewhere in between, but you don’t have to walk alone. We’ll walk this road together.’ That belief shapes every interaction I have and reminds patients they are supported, no matter where they’re starting."

Connection as care: "I approach each patient like they’re a member of my own family. That means listening closely, showing up with empathy, and creating a space where they feel safe to share what’s really going on. I focus on improving women’s daily lives—not just treating symptoms, but helping them feel seen, heard, and better equipped to move through midlife with confidence and support."

Jennifer Bild, MSN, APRN, WHNP-BC

From fertility to midlife: "I began my healthcare career at a fertility clinic, supporting patients through some of their most hopeful and vulnerable moments. As my career evolved, I worked in a maternal-fetal medicine clinic focused on patients with gestational diabetes and at an OB/GYN clinic caring for women across all ages and stages. Those experiences gave me a deep appreciation for how complex and interconnected women’s health can be."

Finding purpose in midlife care: "While working at the OB/GYN clinic, I realized how underrepresented midlife women were in both training and everyday care. I started researching on my own, diving into the evolving science around menopause and hormone therapy. The more I learned, the more I recognized the need and the opportunity to make a real impact for women in this stage of life."

Listening first, treating holistically: "When I meet with a patient, my first goal is for her to feel truly heard. I want every woman to leave our visit with a plan that addresses her most pressing concerns today and a path forward that looks at her health as a whole."

Inspired by women: "What drives me most is the women in my own life who are navigating perimenopause, menopause, and post-menopause, often without the support they deserve. They remind me every day why this work matters and why I am so passionate about helping women feel their best in midlife and beyond."

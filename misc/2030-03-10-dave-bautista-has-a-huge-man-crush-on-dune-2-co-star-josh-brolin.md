Dave Bautista, who reprises the role of Glossu Rabban, revealed at the film's New York City that while he has been starstruck by castmates Timothée Chalamet, Austin Butler, Zendaya and Florence Pugh, he is specifically wowed by Josh Brolin, who returns in Part Two as Gurney Halleck.

"God, I have a man crush, a huge man crush on Josh Brolin," Bautista told People while walking the red carpet. "I love him as a performer, as a human being."

Bautista's connection with Brolin goes all the way back to their shared time in the Marvel Cinematic Universe; Bautista played Drax the Destroyer in the Guardians of the Galaxy trilogy, while Brolin played supervillain Thanos in the Avengers franchise, and their characters finally crossed paths in Avengers: Infinity War and Endgame.

"I've known Josh for years and we really got, I think, close during our conversations, very intimate conversations during our Marvel years," he explained.

# Single-Leg Romanian Deadlift

[exercises to lower your](https://defensecascade.china-akan.com/)

Stand with feet shoulder-width apart. Hold a dumbbell or pair of dumbbells with hands slightly more than shoulder-width apart. Move one foot slightly behind the other, holding it a few inches off the ground. With back flat and abs tight, slowly lower the weight toward front foot and allow free leg to float behind you for balance. Once the weight reaches mid-shin level, push through grounded heel to return to the upright position, and repeat on the opposite leg. Perform 2 to 4 sets of 8 reps. Benefit: "I do this exercise with every golfer," says Roberts. "It teaches you to activate each glute independently, so it's a huge power generator.

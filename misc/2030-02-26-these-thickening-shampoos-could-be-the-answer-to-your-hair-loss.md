What Causes Hair Loss?

“There are many types and causes of hair loss,” says Dr. Kash, however the most common is androgenic alopecia, often referred to as Male Pattern Hair Loss (MPHL) or Male Pattern Baldness (MPB). It’s driven by a hormone called dihydrotestosterone (DHT). It’s a naturally occurring sex hormone, but when levels get too high, can shrink hair follicles and shorten the life cycle of hair (a process called miniaturization), leading to hair thinning and loss. While DHT’s connection to MPHL is clear, “other factors such as low levels of inflammation and oxidative stress have also been shown to be at play,” says Dr. Kash.

Does Shampoo Help Hair Growth?

It’s not exactly straightforward. “When it comes to hair growth products, many dermatologists (myself included) generally recommend leave-on topical products like a solution, oil, or foam over a shampoo,” says Dr. Kash. “This is because the time in contact with the scalp is limited with a shampoo as it is usually rinsed out after a few seconds or minutes. This limits potential effectiveness compared to a topical leave-on product.” Many of the ingredients found in hair growth shampoo, she adds, were studied as topical leave-on treatments, not in shampoo formulas, so there is little to no evidence they have the same effect in a shampoo.

That being said, there can be be benefit to using a hair growth shampoo. “The wrong shampoo can definitely wreak havoc on the scalp and worsen hair loss,” explains Dr. Kash. For instance, if someone has dandruff, but does not use an appropriate anti-dandruff shampoo, the dandruff can get worse, leading to inflammation on the scalp which can “potentially worsen hair loss.” Conversely, someone with a dry scalp who is using a shampoo with harsh surfactants or detergents can make their scalp dryness even worse, which may also worsen hair loss.

Essentially, it’s about using the right shampoo for your scalp and hair, advises Dr. Kash. “If any shampoos lead to scalp irritation, flaking, redness, itching or increased hair loss, it should be discontinued.”

What Is the Difference Between Regular Shampoo and Hair Growth Shampoo?

“A hair growth shampoo tends to be formulated for those with hair loss to try to stimulate hair growth,” says Dr. Kash. “Many have ingredients either to try to locally decrease DHT activity or to prolong the anagen (growing) phase of the hair follicle.” It’s important to remember that the efficacy of these ingredients depends on how much time they have in contact with the scalp, so when using a shampoo for hair growth, Dr. Kash advises keeping it on for several minutes before washing it off to allow the ingredients to penetrate.

What to Look for in a Hair Growth Shampoo

The most important thing to look for in a hair growth shampoo is that it has ingredients “that have at least some evidence that they may promote hair growth (even if the evidence is limited and from a leave-on topical product or oral formulation) such as ketoconazole (this is one of the only ingredients that has evidence in a shampoo formulation that it may promote hair growth in AGA/MPHL), saw palmetto, pumpkin seed oil, rosemary, caffeine, melatonin, and green tea extract,” recommends Dr. Kash. Many other shampoos that don’t contain these ingredients can help hair look fuller and thicker, but won’t necessary promote growth.

Ingredients to Look for

The hard fact of the matter is that preventing hair thinning or boosting hair regrowth is a tricky problem to solve. Even the best hair loss treatments like minoxidil (Rogaine) need to stay on the scalp for hours without rinsing in order to be effective. Some of the best hair growth shampoos have DHT blocking properties, like ketoconazole or saw palmetto, but it should be noted that the research on these alternative treatments is new and relatively limited.

That's why the best shampoo for hair growth and loss is one that supports healthy hair growth by effectively cleansing and moisturizing your hair and scalp, without overly drying or damaging hair. Using shampoo with nourishing ingredients including vitamins, proteins, and collagen can help repair damage and promote the appearance of fuller hair. Look for shampoos that are sulfate-free, as sulfates can strip away the natural oils on your scalp, which healthy hair actually needs to grow long and strong.

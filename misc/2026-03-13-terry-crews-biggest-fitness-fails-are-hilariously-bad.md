Crews says losing his identity as an athlete following his NFL retirement in 1997 was rough. He was an annoying workout partner

Some couples enjoy shared fitness routines, but Crews says he "ruined that a long time ago."

The actor attempted to hit the gym with his wife, but the experience was horrible, he says. King-Crews complained of being too tired to keep up with her husband — and in response, Crews admonished her for not working hard enough.

"No, no, you’re not tired. You don’t want it," he remembers saying. Later, Crews found out the cause of King-Crews' fatigue: She was pregnant with their son, Isaiah.

This was during his "toxic masculinity phase," he says — which, thankfully, he's since recovered from. "You get into that bro thing and everybody’s got to push it to the limits," he says. "Never again."

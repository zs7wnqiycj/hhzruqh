# Mixed-Grip Pullup

The pullup is the ultimate two-in-one muscle builder for bulging biceps and a V-shaped back. This variation, called the mixed-grip pullup, also helps define your midsection. By using an overhand grip with one hand and an underhand grip with the other, you add a rotational component to the exercise, says Alwyn Cosgrove, C.S.C.S., owner of Results Fitness, in Santa Clarita, California.

That is, your core musculature is forced to contract hard to stabilize your torso so your body stays straight throughout the move. The result: abs so solid, she can leave her surfboard at home.

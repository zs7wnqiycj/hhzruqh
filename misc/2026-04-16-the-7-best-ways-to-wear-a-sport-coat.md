# The 7 Best Ways to Wear a Sport Coat

Sport coats work for practically every social situation, from casual cocktail parties to dinners with your in-laws. But a sport coat alone won’t make you stand out—you need to focus on the finishing touches, too.

Here are the best pants, shoes, shirts, and accessories to wear with your sport coat—plus a few extra tips for good measure. Steal inspiration from these stylish men of Instagram.

We also love the look of something unexpected: Wear a cool concert tee, chambray shirt, or flannel for a more unique new look that’s not too far outside your comfort zone.

If you’re a style risk-taker, pair your sport coat with a colored trouser or casual sneakers instead of dress shoes.

Plus, unless you have dress shoes that feel like sneakers, they’re more comfortable than any shoes you’d normally wear with a sport coat.

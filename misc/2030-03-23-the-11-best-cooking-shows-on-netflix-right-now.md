# The 11 Best Cooking Shows on Netflix Right Now

Nothing got us through a tough year quite like the serotonin-boosting power of cooking shows. Culinary-themed entertainment so often helps remind us that even when all seems bleak, we can all share in the joy of watching an elaborately designed cake fall to the ground, a British baker in the midst of a crisis over a trifle...or just Gordon Ramsay being Gordon Ramsay.

Luckily, on a streaming giant like Netflix, you have plenty of series to choose from. There's Street Food, an exploration of the best food you won't find served on white table cloths. There are competitions like Nailed It! which teach us that cooking...simply isn't for everyone. Either way, you have options. And to help you sort through those options, we named the 11 the best cooking shows streaming on Netflix now, served up hot.

The Great British Bake Off

When you think of any Gordon Ramsay show, consider GBBO its exact opposite. The Great British Bake Off is good ole wholesome fun, each season full of charismatic home cooks hoping to bake their way to victory in the tents. You won't witness much discord among competitors, which makes the show and its eight seasons currently streaming on Netflix an unexpected pleasure to watch.

Salt Acid Fat Heat

James Beard Award-winning chef Samin Nosrat believes the key to any great meal involves just four components: salt, acid, fat, and heat. In her 2018 Netflix series, Nosrat puts that to the rest, traveling across the globe to find out how different countries incorporate these cooking essentials. The series only has four episodes, so it's an easy series to binge in one afternoon. (Just don't watch on an empty stomach.)

Street Food

The best food isn't always made in high-end kitchens. In Street Food, we meet the cooks and chefs who work in no-frills kitchens around the world. For those of us who can't travel right now, it's the only show you need to watch.

Nailed It!

Don't expect much from the end products of Nailed It!, many of which look like kindergarten art projects. But that's precisely the fun of the Nicole Byers-hosted show, now in its fifth season. In this series, amateur bakers (like...really amateur) battle it out to replicate elaborate cakes, pies, and other confectionary sweets and so often fail hilariously.

Nadiya's Time to Eat

A much-beloved GBBO winner, Nadiya Hussain, hosts this cooking series in which time is of the essence. Each recipe Hussain offers is optimized for efficiency, so it's fun and actually-replicable for us amateur bakers and cooks.

Cooked

Perhaps one of the most beautifully shot cooking docuseries of all time, Cooked follows food writer Michael Pollan on a quest to look at how cooking has shaped our lives (besides the obvious). Pollan travels the world to look at food through the lens of the four physical elements of air, water, fire, and earth.

Chef's Table

No list of great culinary-themed shows is complete without Chef's Table, each episode focusing on one world-renowned chef and their philosophy on cooking. Featuring the likes of Christina Tosi, Enrique Olvera, and Mashama Bailey.

The Final Table

Netflix's super ambitious The Final Table was intended to be an Olympics-style competition, bringing chefs from all over the world to compete in a 10-episode series. The Final Table wasn't perfect, essentially an overdone concept in the style of Iron Chef and Top Chef, but it's surely always fun watching chefs do what they do best.

Best Leftovers Ever

Well, it's pretty self-explanatory. That said, the show is surprisingly fun, as contestants battle it out to turn sad, cold leftovers into spectacular-looking meals. Our leftovers almost NEVER look this good.

Restaurants on the Edge

Three "experts" travel to failing restaurants in picture-perfect locations in an effort to overhaul their space and menu. It's like a lighter Kitchen Nightmares, and though the changes don't always make sense, the show's just fun for landscape eye-candy.

Crazy Delicious

Taking place in Willy Wonka's chocolate factory, Crazy Delicious tasks three cooks with creating wild dishes, plucked from the forest around them. It's Nailed It in an edible wonderland, and we wouldn't have it any other way.

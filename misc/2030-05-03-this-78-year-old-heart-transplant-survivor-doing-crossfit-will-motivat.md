# This 78-Year-Old Heart Transplant Survivor Doing CrossFit Will Motivate the Hell Out Of You

When you think about reaching your 70s, you probably envision yourself with gray hair, a beer belly, and wrinkly skin.

[Doing CrossFit](https://immunopeptide.xianfengchem.com/)

Stop right there. Old age doesn’t have to slow you down—in life or the gym. Take Bob, the 78-year-old man who warmed the hearts of fitness fans everywhere after CrossFit’s main Instagram page shared a video of him doing a barbell push press at his local gym in California.

His trainer, Cory Celia, originally posted the video and wrote: “This warms my heart so much. This is Bob. He is 78 and has a heart transplant. Bob does Crossfit 3x a week. One of his favorite names is Cory because he has a granddaughter named Cory. I am inspired every time I see Bob and so grateful to have coached the senior class today.”

We were inspired, too. Not only is Bob still going hard in the gym—but he survived a heart transplant, a surgery that comes with some serious risks and intense recovery period. One commenter summed up our thoughts perfectly: “Keep going Bob. Really hope that I can fight that much then.”

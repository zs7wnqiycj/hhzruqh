# We Went Down a Rabbit Hole of Patrick Dempsey’s Most Iconic ‘80s and '90s Fits

Patrick Dempsey has been a Hollywood staple forever. Can't Buy Me Love. Sweet Home Alabama. And, of course, Grey’s Anatomy’s McDreamy, of which he recently made a surprise return to. Just like that good ole TV character nostalgia, Dempsey also has a penchant for making us miss many ‘90s things—especially the outfits.

Dempsey has always had good style. He has worn suits of every shape, gotten very liberal with what a ‘dress shirt’ means, and even as his style has evolved, the quality has remained.

But style evolution isn’t just about the clothes. It’s about the whole picture. The whole vibe. And Patrick Dempsey has always maintained an extremely Patrick Dempsey vibe, especially visible through his hair evolution. He’s had long and short, curly and spiky, fully exposed bedhead, and an abundance of hat experiments. An abundance.

Here, we rounded up some of his finest fits, from the young Patrick Dempsey to the 2021 version. Prepare yourself for the many '80s and ‘90s outfits that we can appreciate but also definitely not bring back into rotation.

1986

Technically this photo is from the ‘80s but that hair is quintessential ‘90s. Doesn’t come as a surprise that Dempsey was ahead of the times on good hair moves.

Fast Times wardrobes are the ones we can take some inspiration from and make ‘em modern. Layering fun patterned shirts and cardigans or denim jackets is solid—even better if you thrifted them.

1987

Layering a trucker jacket over a fancier shirt is a cool move for the fashion inclined. Not to mention, these pants are it—then and forever.

When young Patrick Dempsey does formal, he does formal. This perfectly fitted tux and styled back hair is the pinnacle of “cleans up well.”

1988

In a Shallow Grave pushed Dempsey into a long acting career, but he was just a kid when it all started. And a kid in suspenders!

OK, well I have no idea what’s going on with this outfit. A band collar shirt and a printed vest? Truly an outfit to burn in theory, but young Dempsey naturally makes it look like an expertly curated vintage outfit.

I don’t know why a ringer tee and a leather jacket looks so unexpectedly cool, but it does, and sometimes we don’t have to know why. We just know that it works.

1989

I do think we could’ve done without the tank top layered over the tee, but the head-to-toe white and expert bedhead is worthy of attention and adoption.

Somehow this T-shirt, cardigan, and long jacket feels extremely Chandler Bing but in a good way? Men in layers are good, whether you’re Dempsey, Bing, or neither of the above.

This collar situation is a bit complex, but it helps the whole fit feel more special than a plain navy suit. (Although those have their time and place, of course. But when you're on a red carpet all the time, it’s nice to have simple ways to change it up.)

1990

Wingtip collars are the fanciest of the bunch, and Dempsey managed to make it feel unstuffy with a brown bow tie and slightly tousled hair. Formal made unpretentious.

Well, this is wonderful and please bookmark for the holidays. A turtleneck is a great tool to have in your wearing-with-a-suit rotation, and bonus points if you get one in a fun color that’s still easy to match.

When they say fashion is cyclical, this is what they mean. The loose, silky shirt and oversized pants are quintessentially ‘90s, but they are also reappearing on stylish men today. Take notes.

1991

Wearing a suit vest without a jacket or with a jacket that doesn’t match is generally something we’d keep back in the archives. But if you ditched the vest and opted for the tee under a cardigan, you’d be 2021 approved.

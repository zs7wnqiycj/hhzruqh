Explanation

If you're living with moderate to severe allergic asthma or chronic spontaneous urticaria and haven't found relief from standard treatments, Xolair (omalizumab) might be the answer. Xolair is a biologic therapy designed to reduce symptoms by blocking immunoglobulin E (IgE), a key player in allergic reactions. However, administering Xolair requires specific expertise and monitoring. Here's how to find a specialist clinic for Xolair therapy near me and prepare for your first visit.

Why You Need a Specialist Clinic for Xolair Therapy

Xolair isn't a medicine you pick up at your local pharmacy without a prescription. It is:

Administered by subcutaneous (under-the-skin) injection

Prescribed only when conventional inhalers or antihistamines fail

Monitored for potential side effects such as injection-site reactions or, rarely, anaphylaxis

A specialist clinic—usually run by an allergist, immunologist, or pulmonologist—has the training, equipment, and protocols to:

Evaluate your eligibility for Xolair

Perform skin or blood tests to measure IgE levels

Administer injections in a controlled setting

Monitor you for 2 hours post-injection, especially during early doses

Before you start searching, take note of:

A review of your medical history and confirmation of your diagnosis

Scheduling of future appointments, typically every 2 or 4 weeks

Monitoring Your Progress

Biologic therapies take time to show full effects. To track improvements:

Keep a symptom diary—note any changes in asthma symptoms, hives, itching, or need for rescue inhalers

Bring your diary to follow-up visits

Report side effects such as fever, injection-site pain, or unusual allergic symptoms immediately

When to Seek Immediate Help

Serious reactions to Xolair are rare but can occur. If you develop:

Difficulty breathing or swallowing

Swelling of the face, lips, or tongue

Severe rash or hives beyond the injection site

A feeling of faintness or rapid heartbeat

Call 911 or go to your nearest emergency department right away. Then follow up with your specialist clinic.

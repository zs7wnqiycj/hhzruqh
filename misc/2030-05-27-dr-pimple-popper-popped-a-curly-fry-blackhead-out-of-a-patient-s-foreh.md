# Dr. Pimple Popper Popped a 'Curly Fry' Blackhead out of a Patient's Forehead

Dr. Pimple Popper is back with some serious blackhead pops in one of her most recent uploads.

Blackheads are hair follicles that have become plugged with dead skin and oil.

The last of her patient's blackheads comes out like "curly fry."

In a recent Instagram video, Dr. Pimple Popper is tasked with taking out multiple blackheads on her patient's nose and forehead. These classic pops send spiraling white bits of content out into the world. However, Dr. Pimple Popper — who is really dermatologist and TLC host Dr. Sandra Lee — saves the best one for last.

The video begins with Dr. Lee using her comedo extractor to pop multiple bumps on a patient's nose. The patient appears to have a condition called rhinophyma which causes the under-the-skin blemishes to form. Applying pressure with the extractor, Dr. Lee begins squeezing out the dead skin and oil that's trapped beneath the surface. These pieces come out in multiple curling strips that she has to push away with her tool.

Dr. Lee, however, has saved the best part of the video for last. She explains to her patient that she's moving to other parts of his face that they haven't focused on as much. She finds a good one. The dermatologist takes her comedo extractor and pushes into a forehead blackhead that spills forth curling contents reminiscent of a "curly fry."

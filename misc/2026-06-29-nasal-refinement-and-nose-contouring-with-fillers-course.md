About this lesson

Enhance your skills in nasal refinement and contouring through this comprehensive training video. This advanced video offers a detailed, step-by-step approach for medical professionals, from assessing the frontal and profile views to evaluating the nasal structure from multiple angles. Learn how to identify and address common concerns such as nasal bone thickening, cartilage width, and minor humps with precision. The video covers essential techniques for achieving a smoother, more harmonious profile through strategic volume placement and support. Equip yourself with the expertise to create balanced, natural-looking results that meet your patients’ aesthetic goals. This video combines clinical insight with practical methods to elevate your approach to nasal enhancement, ensuring optimal outcomes and patient satisfaction.

Comprehensive nasal assessment from multiple angles to identify areas for contour improvement.

Techniques for correcting nasal imperfections, including cartilage irregularities and minor humps.

Advanced profile refinement methods to achieve natural, balanced, and aesthetically pleasing results.

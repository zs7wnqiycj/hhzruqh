Baking soda may help relieve itching from bug bites when mixed with water to form a paste and applied directly to the affected area. This inexpensive home remedy works by neutralizing the skin's pH and reducing inflammation, though results vary from person to person.

How to use it: Mix 1 teaspoon of baking soda with a few drops of water to form a thick paste. Apply to the bite, let it sit for 10 minutes, then rinse.

When it works best: Mild bites from mosquitoes, ants, or other common insects.

When to seek help: If itching persists, the bite becomes infected, or you notice signs of an allergic reaction, further evaluation is needed.

While this method is commonly used and can provide some relief, the effectiveness of baking soda in treating itching from bug bites is not well-documented in scientific literature.

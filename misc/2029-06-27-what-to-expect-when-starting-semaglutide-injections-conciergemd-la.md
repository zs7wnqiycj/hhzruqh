What Is Semaglutide and How Does It Work?

Semaglutide is a GLP-1 (glucagon-like peptide-1) receptor agonist, a class of medications originally used to help manage type 2 diabetes. GLP-1 is a natural hormone the body releases after eating, and Semaglutide works by mimicking its effects. According to the Cleveland Clinic, GLP-1 medications help lower blood sugar, reduce appetite, and improve metabolic function.

Semaglutide supports weight loss in three primary ways:

Regulates appetite signals in the brain, helping you feel full sooner and stay satisfied longer

Delays gastric emptying, so food stays in your stomach longer, which reduces hunger

Improves insulin sensitivity, supporting healthy glucose levels after meals

The U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) approved Semaglutide under the brand name Wegovy for chronic weight management in 2021.

# These 9 Brands Are Celebrating Pride Month by Giving Back

Our product picks are editor-tested, expert-approved. We may earn a commission through links on our site. Why Trust Us?

As soon as June hits, it's everywhere. Rainbow this, rainbow that, rainbow everything in stores. It's colorful and cheerful, yes, but the sudden surge of rainbow flags on products (as opposed to at Pride parades or in bars) usually feels like it happens for one big reason: money.

More often than not, the Pride Month rainbow assault comes in the form of brands emblazoning their products with queer symbols in order to appeal to an oft-ignored audience of LGBTQ+ people – or more specifically, those peoples' pockets. Marginalized communities have major buying power when they're actually recognized.

Suddenly, a month of rainbow flags turns into a month of transparent pandering and rainbow gimmicks on the part of brands.

[Giving Back](https://reboundworks.escortskhi.com/)

It all detracts from the true meaning of the month-long holiday: pride. As LGBTQ+ people face mounting threats and traumas in regards to their mental health and physical wellbeing, it's important to remember charities, organizations, and nonprofits that help uplift the community as opposed to exploiting it.

Fortunately, several brands have made it a mission to back their Pride-themed merch with actual funding to these charities, literally putting their money where their mouth is and proving that they understand the meaning behind the flag. These nine brands have each pledged their support for LGTBQ+ people not only by slapping the rainbow onto their clothes, but also by supporting those who celebrate Pride all year long.

Abercrombie Pride Crew Sweatshirt

Abercrombie's Made For Love Collection was created to support The Trevor Project, which provides crisis intervention and suicide prevention services to LGBTQ youth. The line includes sweatshirts, baseball hats, and t-shirts.

Levis Pride Regular Cut Socks

For its fifth Pride-themed collection, Levi's partnered with two different organizations: the Harvey Milk Foundation and the Stonewall Community Foundation. 100 percent of the proceeds from the line of socks, shirts, and jackets will go towards the two foundations.

Under Armour HOVR Sonic Pride Edition Running Shoes

Under Armour's colorful Pride apparel was created in support of Athlete Ally. Together, the two organizations created a curriculum designed to foster an inclusive and enriching environment for all athletes and the communities surrounding them.

H&M Equality Twill Cap

Affordable retailer H&M created its own line of equality-based apparel, including hats, t-shirts, and sweats, to benefit the United Nations Human Rights Office's Free and Equal Campaign. The UN will receive 10 percent of the sale proceeds.

Speedo The One Brief

Though there's only so much color you can print on a small Speedo brief, swimmers can still show their true colors thanks to the iconic brand's limited-edition Pride line. Product, including briefs and goggles, will be given to US Masters' national roster of LGBT swim teams.

American Apparel Still Here T-Shirt

[Are Celebrating](https://allpepay.com/)

American Apparel, which is now an online-only basics brand, released a line of progressive unisex t-shirts in honor of Pride. With their unapologetic slogans, the brand also sent a powerful message by hiring (and therefore compensating) LGBTQ+ models for its lookbook. As it stands, the wage gap sets LGBTQ+ people, especially trans women of color, back in terms of annual salary.

Apple Watch Pride Edition Woven Nylon Band

Apple stepped up its game by making donations to the following advocacy organizations: Gender Spectrum, GLSEN, ILGA, PFLAG, The National Center for Transgender Equality, and The Trevor Project. Alongside the multi-colored band, the company also debuted a new watch face that celebrates the rainbow flag of Pride.

Harry’s Shave with Pride Set

This shaving set is pretty eye-catching considering how loud and proud it is in comparison to the usual razors and shaving tools. Even better: 100% of profits made from its sale will be donated to LGBTQ+ causes, including GLAAD.

Converse Pride x Miley Cyrus Chuck 70 High Top

Consider this a new spin on your old Chucks in the best way possible. Proceeds from the Converse Pride Collection will support LGBTQ+ youth-based charities, including the Happy Hippie Foundation, It Gets Better Project, Minus 18, and RainbowYOUTH.

# Fitness Model Arrested For String of Armed Robberies​

David Byers, a tatted up 34-year-old who had a side job as a model posing for photographer Michael Stokes’s Instagram portfolio, has been arrested in the San Diego area for a string of armed robberies he allegedly committed on the other side of the country, in Connecticut.

The arrest, which went down on Wednesday, came after Byers managed to avoid police in at least four states, including New York, Pennsylvania, Arizona and (of course) Connecticut.

He fled in a stolen Range Rover, which he eventually ditched, and was able to evade cops in Pennsylvania as well.

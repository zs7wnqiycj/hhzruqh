# 36 Funny Perimenopause Quotes, Jokes, and Memes

We all need funny perimenopause quotes in our lives—especially if you find yourself crying at the supermarket and don’t know why, or you’re throwing side-eyes left and right, or you’re just so over it and have no more you-know-whats to give. Menopause jokes are funny because they ring all-too true. But joking aside, there are ways to ease the symptoms that the memes riff on. Learn next steps for finding relief (like getting care from a Midi Health clinician) when the funny menopause quotes hit too close to home.

There’s something about approaching the tough stuff in life with a sense of humor—and that includes perimenopause. When you’re suddenly dripping in sweat (in an air-conditioned room, no less), so irritated you might just up and walk out your door and head into the woods to start a new life, or have had what feels like your millionth case of brain fog, some women find it helps to laugh it off a bit. (And we couldn’t agree more.)

That’s why perimenopause quotes, midlife memes, and menopause jokes can be a true mood-booster. At the very least, they may make you feel validated and remind you you’re never alone in this journey. Plus, when we share perimenopause memes and jokes with one another, it underlines the fact that we’re all going through it as midlife women. In that way, humor becomes about connection—though it’s certainly not a substitute for care.

Here’s to laughing and sharing with each other. Laughter isn’t the only medicine you need, though, so if you have perimenopause symptoms disrupting your daily life that could benefit from support, be sure to talk with a menopause-informed clinician, like one at Midi, about how to get the care you need and finally feel like yourself again.

Is that sweat dripping down your face—or are those tears of frustration? Who can really tell the difference at this point? And if you laugh about thermostat wars with your partner or thoughts of flashing the entire train to relieve the sudden heat, we definitely don’t blame you. “I don’t have hot flashes…. I have short, private vacations in tropical-like conditions!”

There’s no shortage of hot flash jokes out there because hot flashes are one of the most common symptoms of menopause. In fact, 75% to 80% of women report having hot flashes.

While dressing in layers and toting a portable fan with you at all times can help you cool off when the heat rises, there are good treatments, too, so you don’t have to suffer in sweaty silence.

Botanical therapies and non-hormonal or hormonal prescriptions can all help manage hot flashes. No blasting the car’s air conditioning directly at your face again.

While it may feel nice to laugh about, remember: Mood changes are not personality flaws. Your hormones are on a roller coaster, taking your mood along for the ride. Tailored solutions—including hormone therapy, non-hormonal medications, supplements, or lifestyle strategies—can be vital in easing mood symptoms. Sometimes, though, midlife stress and obligations can weigh heavily on your mental health. When that happens, emotional support in therapy or with medication management may be right for you. Talk with a licensed mental health professional or a Midi clinician for guidance unique to you.

Period Chaos Quotes for the Unpredictable-Cycle Chapter

Your period has no cares left to give. It arrives when it wants, stays too late, ghosts you for a while, and may still even gives you pregnancy scares. If you want a dose of laughter to make the process feel more manageable, here are some funny perimenopause quotes to keep at bay:

“Perimenopause periods: the surprise party you never asked for.”

“Just when you get used to PMS, they tell ya you’ve got perimenopause.”

“Perimenopause: the only decade where you can have PMS, anemia, hot flashes, rage, AND still get pregnant. Hormone chaos, meet real life.” –Heather Hirsch, MD

“Some people sneeze and worry they have flu. When I sneeze, I just pray I don’t pee myself.”

Irregular periods are one of the first symptoms of perimenopause—and if yours are so wildly unpredictable that your calendar app is mad at you, consider it the first sign to talk with your clinician and stay on top of your symptoms. If you have red flag symptoms—such as very heavy bleeding, bleeding after sex, or bleeding after menopause—see a clinician soon to rule out concerning causes.

Key Takeaways

Funny perimenopause quotes, memes, and jokes can help you get through the tougher symptoms of menopause.

When we read funny quotes, it’s relatable, and we realize that we’re not alone in our menopause experience.

Although menopause quotes can make us laugh and bring us together, it doesn’t mean you have to grin and bear it through these symptoms.

Seek care from a menopause-informed clinician when menopause symptoms interrupt your quality of life, relationship, or ability to work, or when they may point to additional health concerns.

Can perimenopause memes actually help?

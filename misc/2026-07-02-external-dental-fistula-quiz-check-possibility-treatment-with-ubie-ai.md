What is External Dental Fistula?

A dental fistula is a pathway formed between the oral cavity and the bone surrounding the affected tooth to drain an infection. It is typically found on the gum tissue near the affected tooth. It can look like a pimple in the gums and mainly occurs after an abscess due to trauma or infection.

Typical Symptoms of External Dental Fistula

Pus-filled blisters or bumps on the skin

Toothache

Swelling of the face and lip

Skin problems

Redness, bumps, or irritation on the skin

Swelling in any body part

Treatment of External Dental Fistula

A dentist consultation is necessary. Oral antibiotics may be necessary and frequent rinsing of the mouth with warm water and salt or mouthwash is recommended to help reduce bacteria and maintain proper oral hygiene.

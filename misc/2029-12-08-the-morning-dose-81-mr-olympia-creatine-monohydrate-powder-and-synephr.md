Creatine monohydrate is one of the most researched and effective supplements for boosting strength, power, and muscle growth. This guide explores the top creatine monohydrate supplements available in 2025, highlighting their benefits, potential side effects, and recommended dosages.

Creatine works by increasing phosphocreatine stores in the muscles, helping to rapidly regenerate ATP—the primary energy source for high-intensity exercise. Studies show that creatine supplementation enhances muscle performance, accelerates recovery, and promotes lean muscle mass growth.

While generally safe and well-tolerated, some individuals may experience mild bloating or water retention during the initial loading phase. Synephrine is a powerful stimulant commonly used to enhance metabolism, boost energy levels, and support fat loss. This guide explores the top synephrine supplements available in 2025, highlighting their benefits, potential side effects, and recommended dosages.

Synephrine works by stimulating the adrenergic system, increasing thermogenesis and energy expenditure.

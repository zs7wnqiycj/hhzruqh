What is Intercostal Neuralgia?

Nerve pain caused by damage to the nerves running along the ribs. It can be caused by various factors, including rib injuries and viral infections like Shingles.

Diagnostic Questions for Intercostal Neuralgia

Your doctor may ask these questions to check for this disease:

Does your chest pain feel numb?

Do you have stomach or abdominal pain in a specific area?

Do you have sharp chest pain when coughing or taking a deep breath?

Does pain improve with rest but worsen with movement?

Are you experiencing chest pain?

Treatment of Intercostal Neuralgia

Treatment options include pain and nerve medications, which can be in the form of cream, skin patches, or pills. In severe cases, an injection or surgery can be performed to block the nerve from sending pain signals.

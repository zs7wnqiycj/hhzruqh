# P90X Creator Tony Horton Reveals He’s Still Got Shredded Abs at 63

[Creator Tony Horton](https://immunopeptide.xianfengchem.com/)

Tony Horton is the fitness entrepreneur behind the wildly popular P90X program, and a trainer to stars like Ewan McGregor, so it should come as no surprise to learn that he is in incredible shape at the age of 63. But there's "good for his age" and then there's Horton levels of lean muscle, which he just showed off on social media.

[Abs at](https://bioreg.wiki/56xvwl/)

But he has goals beyond simply looking ripped. In a recent Instagram post, the trainer and author explained that he has spent the last several months upping his workout routine to six days per week, combining resistance training and cardio to ensure he had the necessary strength and endurance in anticipation for skiing over the holidays. He also shared some insight into his approach to training, particularly how he prioritizes consistency over intensity in his workouts.

"Over the course of the last 4 months I really buckled down and trained 6 days a week in preparation for the 2021/2022 ski season," he wrote in the caption. "Most weeks consisted of alternating upper body and lower body routines and cardio sessions. I recently got back from Jackson Hole and Grand Targhee in Wyoming and while I was there I skied 3 days (I took this photo after my 3rd day of skiing) and trained 3 days. One day at Targhee I thought I’d try skiing top to bottom to see if I could do it without running out of gas part way down. To my amazement it was pretty easy. I had never done that before on that run at Targhee."

"As a 63 year old it’s an absolute blast to charge this hard," he continued. "My number one priority is CONSISTENCY. Showing up way outweighs intensity. I have good days and bad days just like everyone else, but as long as I do something (even if I have to back way off) it always pays off."

# Build More Muscle in Less Time With This Game-Changing Supersets Workout

SOME DAYS YOU don’t have time for a workout, but you also can’t keep the gains train rolling without one. That’s when you need a strategy that delivers the same muscle-building results in half the time, which is exactly what this hyper-efficient workout does.

Developed by celebrity trainer Don Saladino, this 30-minute session packs in all the hypertrophy of an hour circulating through the weight machines at the gym but uses only a set of dumbbells. You’ll decrease your rest between exercises to keep your total work time low, but cycle through complementary moves that will push you to your limits without totally burning you out. It’s the most efficient you can be, but it won’t be easy. Luckily, the rewards—increased work capacity, endurance, and muscle—are well worth the effort.

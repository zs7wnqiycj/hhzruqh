Dr. Manhattan returned on Watchmen, and he looked familiar.

Yes, he looked familiar to fans of both the graphic novel and Zack Snyder's film, but he also looked familiar to people who see commercials for cleaning products.

[Clean and Can't Unsee](https://980187.com/ru1uvbenpa/)

Let's just say it: Dr. Manhattan looked like Mr. Clean.

[Bears Striking Resemblance](https://jointrevive.xianfengchem.com/blog/7663883290.html)

Let's start out with the obvious, first: HBO's Watchmen is a triumph. From its on-screen aesthetic, to the way it expands on it's Alan Moore-written source material while also embracing it, to just about every performance, HBO's foray into comic book-influenced material has been just about perfect. But that phrase "just about" does leave us a little bit of wiggle room, particularly the appearance of Yahya Abdul-Mateen II's Dr. Manhattan, who made his on-screen debut last night. Mainly, that he looks like cleaning product legend Mr. Clean. Look, it wasn't a thought I wanted to have, but it happened, and now it must be shared.

Why does Dr. Manhattan look like Mr. Clean? Well, there are key differences, first of all: he is blue. Can't forget that. But also, there's some trait crossovers here: the bald head the first and obvious trait, but the primary reason is actually the T-shirt he's rocking. In other forms, we're used to Dr. Manhattan basically rocking just his birthday suit, so the black crew neck is a bit of a game-changer here. And it's a game-changer that made me feel like I needed to clean my apartment.

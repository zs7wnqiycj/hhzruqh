What to Consider

Accuracy

You'll want a scale that provides consistent readings. Smart scales continue to become more accurate, but keep in mind that they rely on bioelectrical impedance, which has a margin for error (and is based heavily on hydration). That said, they've gotten more effective at giving you a thumbnail sketch of your body composition, and can give you a solid idea of whether you're trending in the right direction or not.

Metrics Tracked

There are way better metrics to keep track of other than weight. The best smart scales will measure other useful data such as body fat percentage, muscle mass, bone mass, and water percentage, all the way up to an accurate ECG (electrocardiogram) reading.

Number of User Profiles

Not that crucial to some, but if you have significant others using the scale, look for a scale that allows you to set up multiple profiles or accounts.

Wi-Fi or Bluetooth Connectivity

In an ever-connected world we live in today, this can be a handy feature. All of the scales we feature below have these capabilities.

App Compatibility

While a good number have their own brand of apps, a nice feature is being able to connect to more widely used ones like Apple Health, Google Fit, or Fitbit. Having this syncing ability will streamline your data into one place, which is crucial for giving you a holistic snapshot. There's nothing more frustrating than having to use multiple apps just to figure something out.

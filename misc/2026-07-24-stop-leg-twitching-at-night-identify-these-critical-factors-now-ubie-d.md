# Stop Leg Twitching at Night: Identify These Critical Factors Now | Ubie Doctor's Note

Published on: 7/14/2026

What factors contribute to twitching of legs during sleep?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Leg twitching during sleep is most often caused by restless legs syndrome (RLS) and periodic limb movement disorder (PLMD). Both conditions are linked to neurological factors, involve involuntary muscle movements, and become more common with age. Other contributing factors may include iron deficiency, certain medications, stress, and underlying nerve issues.

Because nighttime leg twitching can stem from several different causes—some easily managed, others requiring medical attention—it's important to identify what's driving your symptoms before deciding on next steps.

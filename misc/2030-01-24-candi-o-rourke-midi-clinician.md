[Candi O'Rourke Midi](https://repair-cascade.jcesqc8.com/)

Candi O'Rourke, MSN, APRN, WHNP-BC

A career grounded in women’s health: "My healthcare career began in 2002 and transitioned to focus on women’s health in 2010. Over the last 15 years, I’ve spent a lot of time working with adolescents, women experiencing sexual health concerns, obstetrics, and public health. I’ve cared for patients in private practices, corporate and business-owned practices, and government settings, and through it all, my passion for supporting women at every stage of life has stayed steady."

A woman-centered philosophy: "I came to Midi to help women through their menopause journey. The virtual model allows me to reach more women than ever before, meeting them where they are and offering care that feels accessible and personal. My approach to care is simple: it’s entirely woman centered. I’m here to help you find the best path forward and support the goals you have for your health."

Helping women move forward: "When it comes to midlife symptoms, I focus on listening closely, understanding what’s most disruptive in your day-to-day life, and creating a plan that feels both realistic and empowering. My goal is for you to leave each visit feeling understood, supported, and confident that you have a partner in your care."

# 25 Lower-Body Exercises That Are Better With a Mini Band

In my opinion, no lower-body workout is complete without some mini-band work. A mini band is a small continuously-looped band that you wrap above your knees or ankles. It mobilizes your hips, activates your glutes, enhances your performance, and reduces your risk of injury.

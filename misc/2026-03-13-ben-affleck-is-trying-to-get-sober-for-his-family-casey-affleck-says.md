# Ben Affleck Is Trying to Get Sober for His Family, Casey Affleck Says

Last month, Ben Affleck checked back into rehab after a reported intervention from his estranged wife, Jennifer Garner. Affleck has been open about his struggle with alcohol addiction, and when he completed treatment in 2017, he thanked Garner for her support. He had also gone to rehab in 2001.

Most recently, Ben's younger brother, fellow Oscar winner Casey Affleck, spoke openly to Entertainment Tonight about his brother's road to recovery.

"Alcoholism has a huge impact on not just the person, but also their family," he said. "I think for his kids’ sake and for their mom, and for himself, he’s trying to do the work and get it together."

As People reports, Casey then talked about how their family history has impacted both of their lives.

# Fever During Chemo: When to Seek Medical Help Immediately | Ubie Doctor's Note

Published on: 7/21/2026

When to seek medical care for a fever during chemotherapy?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

A fever during chemotherapy is a medical emergency and requires immediate care. Chemotherapy weakens the immune system by lowering white blood cell counts, making even minor infections potentially life-threatening. A temperature of 100.4°F (38°C) or higher may indicate febrile neutropenia, a serious condition that can progress rapidly without prompt antibiotics.

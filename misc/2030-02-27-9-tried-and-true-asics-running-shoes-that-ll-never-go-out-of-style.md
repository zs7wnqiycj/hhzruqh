Cushioning Types Used in Asics Running Shoes

Asics offers a few types of cushioning in their midsoles, each designed to cater to different running styles, preferences, and performance needs. Let's break down some of the key differences between these systems.

GEL®: One of Asics' most renowned cushioning technologies is the GEL® system. Gel inserts, strategically placed in the midsole, act as shock absorbers to dampen impact forces and enhance cushioning. The gel material adapts to the specific shape and movement of the foot, providing responsive cushioning while reducing the risk of injuries. This technology is great for those seeking a balance between comfort, stability, and impact protection.

FlyteFoam®: The brand introduced FlyteFoam® as a lighter weight cushioning solution that maintains responsiveness and energy return. Created using a unique foam formulation, FlyteFoam® midsoles provide more noticeable bounce and cushioning than GEL®, giving that plush and springy feel to the finish line. This technology is ideal for runners desiring a lightweight and highly cushioned shoe that doesn't compromise on durability.

FlyteFoam® Lyte: Building on the success of FlyteFoam®, FlyteFoam® Lyte makes for an even lighter and more responsive midsole. It maintains the high energy return of FlyteFoam®, while reducing the overall weight of the shoe. It's best for runners seeking a nimble and agile shoe with ample cushioning for their daily training or racing needs.

GEL®-Nimbus: The GEL®-Nimbus series is a flagship line within the Asics range, known for its plush cushioning and long-distance comfort. This line utilizes a combination of GEL® cushioning and multiple layers of foam to provide a cloud-like underfoot experience. GEL®-Nimbus models are great for runners seeking maximum cushioning, durability, and support for high-mileage runs.

DynaFlyte™: Combining FlyteFoam® with organic fibers, DynaFlyte™ midsoles offer a comfortable and supportive ride all with a feather-light feel. This technology is particularly suitable for runners who prioritize speed and agility, making it ideal for tempo runs, races, or workouts that require quick transitions. Think high performance only with this one.

How to Choose the Right Asics Running Shoes for You

Asics makes choosing the right running shoes pretty simple by breaking down its offerings into two categories: stability running shoes and cushioning/neutral running shoes.

Stability running shoes are the most supportive, designed to slow down excessive pronation. They incorporate technologies that support stable movement such as inset slope technology and extra midsole reinforcements.

On the other hand, cushioning or neutral running shoes focus on comfort. Asics cushioning shoes, whether stability or neutral, utilize FLYTEFOAM™ technology for a firm bounce, energy return, and excellent cushioning.

Once you know which category you're best suited for, it's important to choose the right running shoe for your gait. There are three types: overpronation, neutral, and underpronation (supination).

Overpronators need stability shoes like the Gel-Kayano, while neutral runners benefit from neutral cushioning shoes like the Gel-Nimbus.

"Asics makes great shoes for all foot types and offers a ton of width options," remarks Dr. Mendeszoon.

Finding the right shoe for your foot anatomy, running style, and preferences takes time, regardless of which brand you're shopping. If you find a model that works for you, don't feel the need to switch it up based on the latest releases or current trends.

"Consumers should be aware that, like with automobiles every year or two, there will be new adjustments and changes to the shoe," explains Dr. Mendeszoon. "Even though the shoe may have the same name, it could change significantly from edition to edition."

If you fall in love with a shoe, Dr. Mendeszoon recommends purchasing a few pairs of that particular model number.

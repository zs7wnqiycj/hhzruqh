# Tom Checketts Is More Than Just the Hot New Chef on 'Below Deck Med'

The following story contains spoilers from the current season of Below Deck Med.

Tom Checketts is the newest cast member on Below Deck Med.

He's the boyfriend to current bosun aboard the Wellington, Malia White.

He went to culinary school and worked alongside some notable chefs.

Between the constant hirings and firings on Bravo's Below Deck series, the current fifth season of Med packs one hell of a drama-filled punch. Recently, fans were introduced to yet another new cast member, after chef Kiko left the show in tears: Tom Checketts.

He's British, charming, good-looking, and has a damn good resume to back up his cooking skills. Plus, he's the boyfriend of this season’s resident bosun, Malia White, which means the drama on the Wellington is only just beginning. But what was the chef up to before his time on reality television?

We know he has plenty of experience on yachts, but he also worked under one especially well-known chef and reality star.

He worked long hours under a celebrity chef.

From the show, we learn Tom began his culinary career in London at 18, working under one of the greatest—and most dramatic—chefs in the world: Gordon Ramsay.

“That was probably almost as stressful as working on Below Deck,” he told Decider. “I used to work 90, maybe 92, 93 hours a week. I used to get paid £12,000 a year. But it was a really really tough environment to work in… My mum, she looked at me and started crying because I was so tired and washed-out.”

Since working for Ramsay, Checketts is now a well-known yacht chef, cooking in some of the most beautiful places across the globe. Among some of the places he’s cooked: the French Alps, Switzerland, Austria and Portugal.

He doesn’t pay any attention to the comments about him on Below Deck Med—but his mom does.

For someone on a massively popular show, Tom isn't so concerned with the social media reactions toward him, his relationship or cooking.

“It’s really weird when my mum calls and asks me if everything’s alright,” he told Decider about his social media presence. “I’m like, ‘yeah, why wouldn’t it be?’ and she’s like, ‘Oh, well, just because of all the comments online.’” I don’t read that. But my mum does and she tries to defend me.”

He’s competed in a very famous fitness competition.

According to his official bio page from Bravo, when Tom is not busying creating exquisite dishes for some of his luxury clients in the Mediterranean, he likes to get in a workout when he can. He’s completed multiple IronMan competitions over the last few years, and when he needs to blow off some steam from all the stress, he likes to run a few laps around the shipyard.

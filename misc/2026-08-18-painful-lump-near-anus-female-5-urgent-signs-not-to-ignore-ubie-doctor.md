Explanation

A painful lump near the anus in females may have several causes. One possibility is an infection, which can lead to an abscess that causes pain, swelling, and sometimes fever. Another cause can be a cyst, like a tailgut cyst or a rectal duplication cyst. These are uncommon congenital conditions that, when they become inflamed or infected, can cause severe discomfort near the anus. In some cases, skin changes like those seen in perianal Bowen's disease—a type of skin issue that can sometimes be a precursor to cancer—may also appear as a painful lump.

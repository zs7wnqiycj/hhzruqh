They say you can never have too much of a good thing, and if you ask us, that definitely applies to cyst extractions.

In a series of new Instagram videos posted Thursday, celebrity dermatologist Dr. Sandra Lee, MD—aka Dr. Pimple Popper—goes to town on a giant double-headed cyst. What, pray tell, is a double-headed cyst, you ask? That would be a cyst that spews gunk from two openings instead of one. Double the pus, double the fun. (We're very sorry we just wrote that sentence.)

In the first of two videos, Lee makes an incision over the cyst and starts to force out the gunk from inside. Soon enough, the gunk starts spiraling out from a second opening.

"It's like the gift that keeps on giving," one fan wrote in the comments section.

In the second video, Lee works to snip out the sac—but not before squeezing out a few last bits of gunk, of course! Dr. Pimple Popper's fans seemed thrilled by the sac removal, an important step in making sure the cyst does not come back. They voiced their enthusiasm in the comments:

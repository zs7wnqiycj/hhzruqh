What treatments are effective for hair loss associated with PCOS?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Treatments for hair loss in PCOS include medications like minoxidil and oral contraceptives, and natural compounds such as saw palmetto and pumpkin seed oil.

Explanation

Hair loss in women with PCOS (Polycystic Ovary Syndrome) can be treated with various options. One common treatment is minoxidil, a topical medication that helps to stimulate hair growth. Oral contraceptives can also be effective as they help balance hormones, which can reduce hair loss.

There are also natural compounds that may help with hair loss. Saw palmetto is a plant extract that some people use to promote hair growth. Pumpkin seed oil is another natural option that might help reduce hair loss.

These treatments can be part of a plan to manage hair loss, but it's important to talk to a healthcare provider to find the best approach for individual needs. If you're experiencing hair loss or other PCOS symptoms, you can check your symptoms online to better understand what might be happening and prepare for your doctor's visit. Remember, these treatments may work differently for each person, so patience and consistency are key.

# Meet The Cast of 'The Circle' Season 3

It feels like yesterday we were eager to find out who won The Circle Season 2. Hot on the heels of that finale, Netflix gave us exactly what we wanted: season 3. On September 8, eight new people dropped everything and locked themselves in a room with no clocks, no social media, and a whole lot of cameras in a chance to win $100,000.

For anyone just joining the show, The Circle follows a group of contestants as they’re isolated in rooms with no other form of communication but a “social media” platform called, you guessed it, The Circle. In The Circle, you can be anyone. Yourself, a coworker, a best friend, or just someone you found online and asked to borrow their profile. The key is to become an “influencer,” aka the most popular player in the game and the person who decides who gets to go home. Whoever is left at the end wins the entire game, and gets $100,000.

And with a brand new cast of contestants, there’s bound to be major twists and turns that’ll make the first season of Netflix’s reality competition look easy. A trailer for the season already has a couple surprises. The new cast consists of various ages and backgrounds, and of course, not all of them are truthful about who they are.

Below are the first eight contestants of The Circle Season 3. The real ones, not the catfish.

Ruksana is a 35-year-old from New Jersey. She’s playing as herself, and is prepared to do whatever it takes to get the prize money. Time will tell if she’s got a decent catfish radar.

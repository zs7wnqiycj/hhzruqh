# Take a Web Tour of the NFL

NFC next week. If you have some fan sites you love, e-mail us at MHonline@rodale.com.

AFC East

Miami Dolphins -- Dolphins Training Center - Davie, FL

Phinfever - Daily fan site.

This is one of the best fan sites we've seen and it's updated every day. Be sure to check the About Us link. They are a colorful collection of guys!

They have cheerleader pictures going back to 2001. You might want to pick up a calendar.

Buffalo Bills -- St. John Fisher College near Rochester, NY

They’ve got nothing, except this sad training camp photo gallery.

New England Patriots -- Gillette Stadium - Foxboro, MA

AFC North

Baltimore Ravens -- McDaniel College - Westminster, MD

Their Playbook Media Center is well organized, although at this point in the preseason, not much new is going on there.

Cincinnati Bengals – Georgetown College - Georgetown, KY

The Bengals, on the other hand, are in full stride with same-day interviews and videos . The “Fear Da Tiger” video puts Chicago’s “Super Shuffle” to shame. While you’re there, check out the Ben-Gals cheerleader team photo. The girls form a living optical illusion.

Cleveland Browns -- The Cleveland Browns Training and Administrative Complex - Berea, Ohio

You need to download their 6-page coloring book!

Pittsburgh Steelers -- Saint Vincent College - Latrobe, PA

For being world champs, the selection was mighty thin. Steelersfever.com

did lead us to a handy list of bars, for not just Pittsburgh, but the whole USA and some international locales too, in case you want to watch the game with some friends.

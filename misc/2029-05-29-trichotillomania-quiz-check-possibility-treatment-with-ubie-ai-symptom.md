What is Trichotillomania?

A psychological disorder where a patient feels a strong, irresistible urge to pull out their hair. It can be caused by anxiety, stress, or simply as a habit built up over time.

Typical Symptoms of Trichotillomania

Hair loss

Area of bald skin with unclear edges

Mental stress and physical fatigue

Skin problems

Diagnostic Questions for Trichotillomania

Your doctor may ask these questions to check for this disease:

Are you experiencing increased hair loss?

Do you have an area of bald skin with unclear edges?

Are you experiencing mental stress and physical fatigue?

Treatment of Trichotillomania

This is primarily treated with topical steroids to decrease any component of itch, and behavioral therapy and redirecting the urge to pull hair. Oral medication may help in severe cases.

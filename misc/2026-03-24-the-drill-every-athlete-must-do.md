# The Drill Every Athlete Must Do

[Drill Every Athlete Must](https://metabolic-horizon.bbsxyxy.com/blog/1737620113.html)

[Every Athlete Must Do](https://mendstride.snaketongueofficial.com/)

Sports don't happen in slow motion. So why would you work out that way? Todd Durkin, C.S.C.S., author of The IMPACT! Body Plan, trains clients like New Orleans running back Darren Sproles using high-speed drills. One of Durkin's favorites: Jumping rope 500 times as fast as possible. "Jumping rope may appear easy, but you need to be in great shape and extremely quick and light on your feet to complete 500 fast-paced repetitions without stopping," he says. Do this drill often enough, and you'll improve your conditioning and coordination, and crush the competition.

Ready to give the drill a shot for this weekend's fitness challenge? Grab a jump rope and a stopwatch, and then watch the video above. Durkin completed 500 jumps in 3 minutes and 58 seconds. When Sproles performed the drill, he completed 500 reps in 3 minutes and 20 seconds. What was your time? Let us know in the comments below.

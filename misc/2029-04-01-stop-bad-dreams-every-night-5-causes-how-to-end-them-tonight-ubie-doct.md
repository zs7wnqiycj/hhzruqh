Bad dreams, or nightmares, are commonly caused by stress, anxiety, depression, PTSD, and neurodevelopmental conditions like ADHD. They can also be triggered by sleep deprivation, certain medications, or trauma. Reducing stress, improving sleep hygiene, and seeking treatment for underlying mental health conditions can significantly reduce their frequency.

Because bad dreams can stem from many overlapping causes, identifying the specific trigger is the fastest path to relief. Explanation

Bad dreams, or nightmares, can happen for different reasons. One common cause is stress, which can make bad dreams more frequent. People who are stressed during the day might have more nightmares at night. Another reason could be mental health issues, like anxiety or depression. Some people with Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD) also report having more nightmares.

Ways to Help Stop Bad Dreams

Reduce Stress: Try relaxing activities like reading or listening to music before bed.

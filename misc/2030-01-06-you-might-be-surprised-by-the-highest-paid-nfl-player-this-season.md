Who was the highest-paid NFL player in 2019?

Russell Wilson was the highest-paid NFL player for the 2019 season, and it's likely that he'l keep that title for the 2020 season. The Seattle Seahawks quarterback signed a four-year, $140 million contract extension in April 2019, and the contract set records in the NFL for average salary per season at a whopping $35 million. Forbes also reports that Wilson earned $9 million for endorsements, as he's sponsored by companies including Alaska Air Group, Bose, and Nike.

Who is the highest-paid NFL player of all time?

Eli Manning, the New York Giants quarterback that announced his retirement just days ago, has logged about $252.3 million in earnings over 16 seasons, making him the highest-paid NFL player of all time.

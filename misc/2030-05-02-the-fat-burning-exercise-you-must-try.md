If you want to burn more fat, improve your fitness, and ramp up your metabolism, try doing kettlebell jumps at the end of your workout. While there are a variety ways that you can use this exercise, we like the approach offered up by Men’s Health fitness advisor Bill Hartman, PT, CSCS. Simply do a kettlebell jumps quickly for about 10 seconds—interspersed with about 50 seconds of rest—for a total of 8 to 10 minutes. This is sort of like doing eight to ten 100-yard sprint intervals, but without the need for a track. That makes this kettlebell jump routine great for burning calories during your workout as well as boosting your post-exercise metabolism.

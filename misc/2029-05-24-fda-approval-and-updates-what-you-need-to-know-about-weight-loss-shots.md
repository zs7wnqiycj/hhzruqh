Wegovy (based on semaglutide) is an FDA-approved drug for weight loss. It is a GLP-1 receptor agonist, a class of weight loss drugs that mimic a natural hormone in the body to regulate food intake. When administered, Wegovy activates GLP-1 receptors in the brain, delaying gastric emptying and reducing appetite, which can lead to decreased calorie intake and weight loss. It should be used alongside a reduced-calorie diet and increased physical activity for effective weight management.

The Wegovy injection is FDA-approved for chronic weight management in eligible individuals 12 years and older. It can be prescribed for adults with obesity (BMI of 30 kg/m² or higher) or overweight (BMI of 27 kg/m² or higher) with weight-related health issues like high blood pressure, type 2 diabetes, or high cholesterol. For pediatric patients (12 years and older), Wegovy is approved for those who classify as obese (BMI 95th percentile or higher for age and sex).

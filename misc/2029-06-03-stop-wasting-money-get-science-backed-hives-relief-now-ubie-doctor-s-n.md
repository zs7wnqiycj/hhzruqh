Health Risks of Unvalidated Treatments

Unproven therapies may do more than drain your wallet. Potential harms include:

Allergic ReactionsHerbal or supplement ingredients can trigger new allergic responses, aggravating hives.

Drug InteractionsSome "natural" remedies interfere with prescription antihistamines, leading to reduced effectiveness.

Misdiagnosis and Delayed CareRelying on unverified practitioners can delay the diagnosis of serious conditions (e.g., auto-immune disorders).

Psychological TollRepeated failures undermine confidence, increase anxiety, and may lead to depression.

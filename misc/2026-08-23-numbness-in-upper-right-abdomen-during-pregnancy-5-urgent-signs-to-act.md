What might cause a numb feeling in the upper right abdomen during pregnancy?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Numbness in the upper right abdomen during pregnancy is often caused by nerve irritation or compression as the body changes. Stretching skin, an expanding uterus, and shifting organs can press on nerves in this area, leading to reduced sensation. Hormonal shifts and fluid retention may also contribute to nerve-related symptoms during pregnancy.

While nerve-related numbness is often harmless, upper right abdominal symptoms in pregnancy can sometimes signal conditions involving the liver, gallbladder, or preeclampsia—so it's important not to guess. Explanation

[Ubie Doctor's](https://aegispeptide.snaketongueofficial.com/)

During pregnancy, your body goes through many changes that can stretch or put pressure on nerves. This pressure might affect the small nerves near your ribs and upper abdomen. When the nerves are squeezed or irritated, they can cause a feeling of numbness or tingling. This is similar to how a limb might "fall asleep" when a nerve is compressed. As your belly grows and you gain weight, the extra pressure can make these nerves feel a bit "off." Sometimes, the nerves that run along the ribs (often between the ribs and the muscles) might get a little irritated because of the changes in your body. It usually is not a serious sign, but your doctor can check things out to be sure everything is okay. If you notice other symptoms like increased pain, swelling, or fever along with the numbness, you can check your symptoms with a free AI symptom checker and be sure to tell your healthcare provider so they can help you feel better.

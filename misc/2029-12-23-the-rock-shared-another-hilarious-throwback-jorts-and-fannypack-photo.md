# The Rock Shared Another Hilarious Throwback Jorts and Fannypack Photo

Before he was known simply as The Rock, Dwayne Johnson tried out a few other alter egos on his way to dominating the world of pro wrestling. One of those in-ring personas, he revealed in the caption of a new Instagram photo today, was “Flex Kavana.”

To his credit, the Rock is quick to acknowledge that the nickname needed work. “Hey, I thought it was cool at the time,” he writes. “And I was making a GUARANTEED $40 bucks per match. I was broke as hell, passionate and gave it all I got—having NOOO IDEA the long, windy, unpredictable road that lay ahead.”

The image shows a Polaroid, addressed to a young fan named Mitchell, urging him to "FLEX!" In the photo, The Rock has his arm slung around Mitchell while donning his signature '90s fannypack over a pair of jorts. The look echoes his famously meme-worthy throwback photo from 1994, which Johnson recreated in 2017 before hosting Saturday Night Live. Understandably, it seems to have put The Rock in a bit of a nostalgic mood—but as always, his self-deprecating sense of humor remained in tact.

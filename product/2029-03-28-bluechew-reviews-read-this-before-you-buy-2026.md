Are combination therapies more dangerous?

In theory, combination therapies should be more dangerous than monotherapies, especially if they contain the same doses you’d normally take for an individual treatment. It makes sense: a greater variety of medications taken at the same doses and all at the same time should have a higher degree of risk associated with it.

The problem with supporting that claim is that most of the specific combinations of medications and doses seen in ED multitherapies haven’t been studied. So we’re left assuming, which is never the best place from which to make medical decisions.

However, we aren’t completely without good information here. There is some research looking at sildenafil combined with tadalafil that shows a comparable side effect profile to either on its own. Specifically, one combination study looked at adding 50mg of as-needed sildenafil to a regimen of 5mg daily tadalafil.28 Not only did the combination outperform monotherapies, but it had the same incidence of adverse events, showing no increase in risk.

Of course, this doesn’t take into account that BlueChew’s multitherapies are all intended for as-needed use only, or that the doses involved tend to be higher and include other medications, namely apomorphine and oxytocin.

Ultimately, it’ll be up to you and your consulting physician to determine whether you’re a good candidate for a multitherapy.

When to see your doctor

In most clinical studies, common side effects, like headaches and flushing, tended to go away with regular use. If these or other side effects persist for you, we suggest talking to your doctor about an alternative PDE5 inhibitor; you might end up switching from sildenafil to vardenafil or tadalafil, for instance.

If you experience any cardiovascular symptoms, especially those related to dangerously low blood pressure — lightheadedness, dizziness, confusion, or significant nausea — you should discontinue use and call your doctor to rule out your medication as a cause.

Nutritional supplements

The supplement industry has been trying to help men improve their performance in the bedroom for decades. As the science and manufacturing processes have evolved, some even appear as though they could make a real difference, at least in milder cases of ED. However, there are some serious caveats to consider before trying a natural ED supplement, as many cause similar cardiovascular side effects as BlueChew’s prescription options but have been less rigorously studied.

If you’re thinking about reaching for a supplement because it seems safer, you might want to reconsider. There’s much more credible research into the exact doses of BlueChew’s ED medications than there is into any one ED supplement ingredient, let alone the blends that contain them.

Nutritional supplements that may help with ED are generally divided into three subcategories: male enhancement pills, testosterone boosters, and volume pills. Let’s take a quick look at each category to understand it a bit better.

Male enhancement pills

These are the most likely to make a difference for men with mild ED, though that likelihood is still relatively small. They contain ingredients like L-arginine, ginseng, and horny goat weed, studies into which are often inconclusive or statistically insignificant, even if they show promise for erectile performance.17 18 If you’re a candidate for BlueChew, we believe you will be better off trying BlueChew than these supplements.

Testosterone boosters

Since low testosterone can contribute to ED, taking a supplement with ingredients that have boosted testosterone in studies might help.19 Some also include ingredients that can help with erectile performance, like the maca in Innerbody Labs Testosterone Support.

Volume pills

Volume pills are as connected with fertility as they are with ED, and many are advertised as being able to increase the quantity and quality of your ejaculate.

Caffeine

Fatigue is a known contributor to low libido and ED. Regular caffeine consumption is also associated with a decreased risk of ED.42 43 If a little bit of caffeine can give you an extra jolt of confidence and alertness when you need it most, it could be worth considering, especially if you need that boost earlier in the day, when a little extra caffeine won’t be as likely to disrupt sleep.

Certain vitamins and minerals

It’s also worth noting that some vitamins and minerals have been associated with ED and occasionally even been shown to help treat it. For example, a 0.1mg dose of vitamin D3 is was shown to improve the performance of 5mg tadalafil in a study comparing tadalafil with vitamin D to tadalafil without vitamin D.21 And a 500mcg dose of folic acid was successful in a recent study looking at its effect on peripheral and penile homocysteine levels, which have been associated with cardiovascular issues often resulting in ED.22

Penis pumps

Penis pumps have been misrepresented in the media over the years to imply that they exist to enlarge a man’s penis. Some less reputable pump manufacturers have jumped on this misconception and have decided to perpetuate it in the hopes that it will help with sales. But pumps are not designed to make a penis any bigger — they’re a treatment for ED.20

Penis pumps work as you might imagine. You insert your penis into a tube that seals against your body. A pump of some kind — often manual, but sometimes automatic — progressively draws air out of the tube, creating a gentle vacuum. That vacuum pressure causes blood to rush to your penis, resulting in an erection when successful. For increased comfort and efficacy, many pumps are designed to be used with water in the vacuum chamber.

For most men, penis pumps are too cumbersome, too difficult, and too inconvenient for practical use, especially for those with active sex lives. ED Rings

ED Rings are extremely simple devices that apply pressure around the base of the penis to help maintain an erection. They do so by constricting the blood flow out of the penis. The great thing about ED rings is that they’re one-time investments without the potential side effects associated with prescription medications.

As such, we would recommend ED rings for men whose cardiovascular health precludes them from trying PDE5 inhibitors like those from BlueChew. Lifestyle adjustments

No one likes to be told that their problems can be solved with diet and exercise. And life doesn’t always permit us to eat our healthiest and exercise regularly.

But those two things — diet and exercise — can sometimes play a big role in erectile function. Increased body fat tends to cause constrictions in blood flow, which directly affect erectile health. A stronger heart pumping blood through less constricted vessels should improve your erections.

There are other lifestyle adjustments you can make to help address erectile dysfunction, as well. For example, we know that stress can cause ED, so reducing stressors, meditating, and even seeking talk therapy can help treat ED at the root.

Fatigue is another potential root cause of ED. Getting proper sleep is the first step to addressing fatigue, but the other considerations discussed here — diet, exercise, stress — can all influence your fatigue levels, as well.

If you drink too much, you can cut back. If you smoke, you should stop. Most of the things you could do to improve your overall health and quality of life will also have a positive effect on erectile function.

# Flu vs Pneumonia Quiz: Compare Symptoms & Treatments

Overview

Flu and pneumonia both affect the respiratory system, but they differ in cause and severity. The flu is a viral infection with a sudden onset of symptoms like fever, body aches, and fatigue. Pneumonia can be viral, bacterial, or fungal, and involves inflammation of the lungs, often leading to cough with phlegm, chest pain, and difficulty breathing. While flu symptoms are more systemic, pneumonia directly impacts lung function and can follow a case of the flu.

Disease Summaries

[Pneumonia Quiz Compare](https://benpepsi.com/)

Flu: Flu is a respiratory infection caused by the influenza virus, commonly affecting the nose, throat, and lungs.

Pneumonia: Pneumonia is a serious infection that causes inflammation of the air sacs in one or both lungs, which may fill with fluid or pus. The condition can range from mild to life-threatening, with symptoms including chest pain, difficulty breathing, fever, and cough with phlegm. While it can affect anyone, pneumonia is most serious in infants, older adults, and people with weakened immune systems.

Treatment Approaches

Flu Treatment Approaches

Antiviral medications for the flu can lessen symptom severity and promote quicker recovery. To be effective, these flu antiviral medications should be taken within the first 48 hours of illness.

Pneumonia Treatment Approaches

Treatment includes antibiotics for bacterial pneumonia, antiviral medications for viral cases, and supportive care including rest and hydration. Over-the-counter medications can help manage fever and pain, while breathing exercises aid recovery. Hospitalization may be necessary for severe cases requiring oxygen therapy or IV medications.
